# Ingot slider: Eclipse-style slider programs (1st-generation wedge coupe look)

A brass slider shaped like a stylised 1st-gen Eclipse: wedge nose with recessed pop-up headlight lids, twin hood
vents, long low hood, raked windshield, narrow greenhouse, long hatch glass (outlines engraved) and a rear wing. It fits the same base and
magnets as the ingot slider (19.6 × 45, 9.5 tall at the roof). How to set up and run: ../RUN.md (same bar, shims,
vise, Find stock and Find bores as the ingot slider). Batch order:

1. `eclipse-first.nc` on the first raw blank (op 1 only, left station, G54; bar on the two 1.67 mm shims).
2. `eclipse-cycle.nc` once for every blank after it (op 1 left + op 2 right; T99, T24, T11, T1: 4 tool changes).
3. `eclipse-last.nc` for the last blank (op 2 only, right station, G55).

New in op 2: **T20, the 2 mm tapered ball** for the car tops, 24 000 rpm, F1000. ASSUMED (tell me the real
numbers and I'll re-post): carbide, R1 tip, 7.5° per side, 1/8 in shank, 12 mm flutes, 18 mm stick-out. The ball
also cuts the 0.6 x 45° shoulder all round (lower edge Z5.6, tip Z5.3, over the jaws). Recess walls (vents, lids)
keep the ball's R1 blend at their foot.
