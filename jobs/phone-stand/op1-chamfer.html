Phone stand op 1: chamfer restart

For a part that op 1 has already cut (underside up at Z34, 74 × 74 walls, recess and two Ø8 bores done) after the machine was re-homed and G54 can't be trusted. Leave the part clamped as it is. Z0 stays the Vise reference.

  1. T99 touch probe: load it, it is measured on the setter.
  2. Find stock 74 × 74, KEEPZ: tap the stylus, jog over the middle of the part a few mm above it, Continue. The top touch lands on the recess floor (Z32): the top only warns. The sides are touched 8 mm below it, on the finished walls. Size within 1 mm.
  3. Find bores runs by itself: the two Ø8 bores at X∓18 Y19.25, 3 mm below the recess floor. Sets X/Y exactly; stops if their spacing is off by more than 0.1 or the part is turned more than 0.2°.
  4. T1 90° chamfer mill (stick-out 22): 0.5 mm chamfers on the underside edges, the recess and the bores, the same moves as op 1.

About 4 min. Home the machine (Ref All Home) before Cycle Start.
