- 2026-11-15: Jonas wants a gift he can machine in batches on the DMC2.
- Stock he wants to use (Jonas, 2026-11-15): 1x2x4 in or 1x1x8 in bar (aluminum assumed).
- Ideas offered fitting that stock: heavy desk bottle opener (1x1x4), twin tealight holder (1x2x4), desk valet tray, pen block, business-card holder, magnetic stacking cubes (1x1x1), headphone hook, cable block. None chosen yet.
- Jonas (2026-11-15): brass is welcome too; gifts can be multi-part assemblies and include 3D printed parts.
- 2026-11-15: Jonas picked the aluminum + brass magnetic fidget (click-clack) slider. All metal: nothing 3D printed for it. Concept renders and Blender (bpy) scripts in project files gift-ideas/.
- 2026-11-15 FINAL choice (Jonas, after briefly considering the flat classic-rail variation): variation 4, the "ingot": brass top with ~3 mm chamfers like a gold bar on a railed aluminum base, custom patterns engraved on the brass top (dimples, knurl, names, chevrons). He accepts the flip since magnet pockets need a second side anyway.
- Proposed sizes (mine, not yet confirmed by Jonas): base 50 x 25 x 9 mm from 1x1x8 aluminum, 20 mm wide x 3 deep channel; brass slider 50 x 19.6 x 8 mm; 3 magnets 6 x 3 mm per half. Plan: brass op 1 = bottom (face, magnet pockets, straight walls ~4.6 mm), op 2 = flip gripping those walls, then top face, chamfers, engraving.
- Brass stock: Jonas only buys Prime-eligible sizes (Amazon listing, 2026-11-15). Recommended 3/8 x 1 x 12 in ($25.99, about 5 sliders per bar).
- Batch plan proposed 2026-11-15 (mine, unconfirmed): only two tools (1/4 flat + chamfer mill), so 8 x 3 mm magnets (helical bore with the 1/4); saw each bar once into 6 in pieces, 3 parts per piece in the vise; both parts op 1 = bottom (face, walls, pockets on brass), op 2 = flip and grip the finished walls. Catch: 3/8 brass exposes only 4.8 mm above the jaws, so walls come out ~0.5 mm too short to flip cleanly: needs a ~1 mm shim under the bar in op 1 (or 1/2 in brass). Aluminum base wants 1/2 x 1 flat bar rather than 1x1. Vise jaw width vs 6 in pieces not yet checked against vise.step.
- Two-station plan proposed 2026-11-15 (mine, unconfirmed): jaws are 95.5 mm long so blanks are ~100 mm (bar sawn in thirds), 2 parts per blank; station 1 runs op 1 on a raw blank while station 2 runs op 2 on the flipped blank from the previous cycle, one program per material, 2 finished parts per cycle. See [[dmc2-vise-geometry]].
